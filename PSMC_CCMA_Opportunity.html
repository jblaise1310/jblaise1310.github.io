A fully funded opportunity to earn a nationally recognized credential and a pay increase while advancing your career in healthcare.

Eligibility Requirements

To enroll, you must meet at least one of the following NHA eligibility pathways:

PATHWAY 1

Training Program

Completion of a Medical Assistant training or education program within the last 5 years.

PATHWAY 2

Work Experience

At least 1 year of supervised MA work experience in the last 3 years, OR at least 2 years of supervised MA work experience in the last 5 years. Work experience must be in the health field covered by the NHA CCMA exam.

Staff who do not meet at least one pathway are not eligible to enroll at this time. Your manager will confirm your eligibility before enrollment is finalized.

Your Path to Certification

Review and sign your documents

Read through your Staff Acknowledgement and Employee Success Plan. Sign and return both to your manager.

Get enrolled

Once your eligibility is confirmed and documents are returned, PSMC will register you and provide access to your NHA study materials.

Study at your own pace

Use the suggested 12-week guide in your Success Plan as a framework. Work through the NHA domains on your own schedule, outside of work hours.

Check in monthly

Meet briefly with your manager each month. These check-ins are here to support you and keep your target exam date in sight.

Schedule and sit your exam

Once you feel ready and have hit the practice exam benchmark, schedule your NHA CCMA exam online or at a PSI testing center.

Earn your credential and your raise

Pass the exam, submit your credential for verification, and your pay increase goes into effect. You are a Certified Clinical Medical Assistant.
