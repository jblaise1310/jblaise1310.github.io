How the C-SSRS Screen Opens & Branches in Cerner

Quick Reference
1

When the form opens

Questions 1, 2, and 6 are white and active right away. All three should be completed first.

Q1Have you wished you were dead or wished you could go to sleep and not wake up?
Q2Have you actually had any thoughts about killing yourself?
Q6Have you ever done anything, started to do anything, or prepared to do anything to end your life?
↓Question 2 is a branch point↓

Q2 = No

Questions 3, 4, and 5 stay hidden. They are not needed.

Q2 = Yes

Questions 3, 4, and 5 unlock and must be completed.

Q3Have you been thinking about how you might do this?
Q4Have you had these thoughts and had some intention of acting on them?
Q5Have you started to work out or worked out the details of how to kill yourself? Did you intend to carry out this plan?
2

Question 6 has its own branch point

Q6 is answered up front with Questions 1 and 2. If Q6 is Yes, one more field unlocks.

↓Q6 branch↓

Q6 = No

No further fields appear for this question.

Q6 = Yes

The follow-up field unlocks and must be completed: "Was this within the past 3 months?"

3

Determining the patient's risk level

Each color has a meaning. Work down the questions in order. The last question where the patient answered Yes sets the color, and that color is their risk level.

Yellow = Low
Orange = Medium
Red = High
Good to know: The yellow, orange, and red bands next to each question are not something staff select. They auto-populate as a triage indicator based on the answer given. They exist to help you recognize risk level at a glance, not as an additional field to complete.
