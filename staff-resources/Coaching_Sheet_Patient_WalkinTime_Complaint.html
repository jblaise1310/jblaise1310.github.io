Coaching Sheet: Patient Walk-in Time Complaint

Department: RHC
Role: Patient Services Representative
Date Sent: August 21, 2026
Coaching Provided By: Jo Blaise, Manager: Clinic

Situation Summary

A patient arrived for a scheduled appointment at 8:30 a.m. and missed being seen due to registration processing time. The patient was directed to the walk-in clinic. When she attempted to check in, she asked the Patient Services Representative how long the wait would be. The representative told her "it's going to be a long time" and that they were "booked all day," without offering a specific timeframe or checking with clinical staff first.

A nurse was pulled in to clarify wait times and informed the patient she could return at approximately 12:45 p.m. When the patient referenced what she had been told about being "booked all day," the representative denied having said it, responding "I never said that" in a raised voice in front of the patient.

The patient described the interaction as unprofessional and left the encounter frustrated, feeling she had been given inaccurate and dismissive information.

Behavior Observed

  • Provided a vague, non-specific response ("it's going to be a long time," "booked all day") instead of checking actual availability before answering
  • Did not escalate to the Lead RN or Team RN to get an accurate wait time before speaking with the patient
  • When the patient repeated back what she was told, responded defensively and denied it in a raised voice, rather than de-escalating
  • Did not offer the patient a concrete alternative (e.g., the 12:45 p.m. return time) until a nurse intervened

Impact

  • Patient left the interaction feeling dismissed and unheard
  • Vague answers created confusion and required a nurse to step in to resolve something that could have been handled at registration
  • Raised voice and denial in front of the patient escalated the situation and damaged trust
  • Contributed to a formal complaint

Expectations Going Forward

  1. For any missed appointment, check with the Lead RN or Team RN before responding to the patient. Do not tell a patient about wait times, availability, or next steps until this has been confirmed. Do not guess or estimate.
  2. Use a simple, appropriate response while checking. For a missed appointment, the response should be: "I'm sorry your appointment was missed. Let me check with the nurse and I'll get back to you." Then follow up with the nurse, find out what can be done, and inform the patient of the outcome.
  3. Never raise your voice with a patient, under any circumstance. If a patient repeats or questions something that was said, respond calmly and clarify. A raised voice is not acceptable, even if the patient is upset or mistaken about what was said.
  4. Give specific, actionable information. Avoid vague phrases like "a long time" or "booked all day." Once confirmed with the nurse, give the patient a concrete time or next step (e.g., "The next opening is around 12:45 p.m.").
