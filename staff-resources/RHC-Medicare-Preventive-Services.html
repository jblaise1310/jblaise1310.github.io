Important Medicare note

For several services (especially lung cancer screening, glaucoma screening, AAA screening, hepatitis screening, and obesity counseling), coverage depends on meeting specific risk-factor requirements in addition to the diagnosis code. AWVs (G0438/G0439) themselves are generally not diagnosis-driven and are commonly billed with Z00.00 or Z00.01 for tracking purposes.

Coverage rules, frequencies, and codes change. Use this as a quick reference and verify current details against CMS or the payer before billing. Coding and billing are handled by the provider and billing team.
