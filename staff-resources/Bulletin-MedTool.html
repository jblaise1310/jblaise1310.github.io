New Tool: Clinic Medication Reference & Study Guide

A quick-reference and learning resource for every medication stocked in our Pyxis cabinets — built for Medical Assistants & RNs.

Now available · June 2026

We’ve added a new resource to help you learn and safely administer the medications we stock. It covers all 112 items in both clinic Pyxis cabinets — what each one is, what it’s used for, how it’s given, and the key things to watch for.

Open the Medication ToolReference & study guide · opens in your browser
Open at: https://jblaise1310.github.io/staff-resources/Clinic_Medication_Reference.html
Getting started
  • Open it in any web browser on your phone or computer — no app or login needed. Just tap the Open the Medication Tool button above.
  • Tip: bookmark it or add it to your phone’s home screen so it’s one tap away between patients.
Two ways to use it
Browse — look something up
  • Search any medication by name, brand, class, or condition.
  • Filter by drug class, by what it’s used for, or by clinic location.
  • Each card shows the generic & brand names, route, class, what it treats, and a “Key point” for safe administration.
Study — learn it (phone-friendly)
  • Flashcards — flip to test recall; pick a category or the “Tricky pairs” deck.
  • Quick quiz — 10 mixed questions; starts with safety must-knows.
  • Safety quiz — high-alert traps specific to our cabinet.
  • Brand ↔ Generic speed match — beat the clock matching trade and generic names.
Built for safety
  • High-value / Prior-Auth meds (the knee viscosupplements) are clearly flagged — always confirm the prior authorization before administering.
  • Look-alike & route reminders are built in — for example the different epinephrine forms, Depo-Medrol vs. Depo-Provera, and which vaccines are subcutaneous vs. IM.
  • Every card carries a “Key point” administration reminder.
Please remember: this is an educational aid — not a substitute for the package insert, clinic protocols, or the provider’s order. Always verify dose, route, and indication before giving any medication.
