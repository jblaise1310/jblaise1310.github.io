A week of cheer, made for you

To thank you for an amazing year, we are celebrating with five days of festive dress, daily treats in the breakroom, and quick ways to connect with each other. Here is everything you need to plan ahead. Everything is optional, so join in as much or as little as you like!

day by day

Get ready: Dig out your most festive pair now. Stripes, snowmen, reindeer, the louder the better.

Wear

Treat

Keep it patient-ready

  • Festive scrubs should be clean, professional, and comfortable for a full day of care.
  • Hats and antlers come off for procedures or any time they get in the way.
  • Treats are grab-and-go in the breakroom all week.
  • Save your ugly sweater for HR's facility-wide event. This week is all about socks, hats, and antlers!
