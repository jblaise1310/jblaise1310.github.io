The old way

  • Having to hunt down the sign-up sheet
  • Handwriting that's hard to read (sorry, Dr. Stopher-Mitchell)
  • Whoever has the sheet has the only copy
  • No way to tell who's covered until you find it

The new way

  • One shared Excel file, open on any clinic computer
  • Type your first name in the box under the date
  • Everyone sees the same calendar, always current
  • Open shifts are obvious at a glance

Sign-ups are due by the 15th of each month. Feel free to click the tabs at the bottom of the file to schedule days for months ahead, whenever it's convenient for you.

Why it's better

Nothing to search for

The calendar lives in one file, in one place. No more asking around for who has the document.

Always up to date

One shared file means one version of the truth. What you see is what's actually scheduled.

Coverage at a glance

A built-in summary tallies shifts per provider and flags any open weekday automatically.

Print it if you want it

Each month is ready to print on one page, if you'd rather post a copy or keep one on your desk.

How to claim a shift

1

Open the shared file

Find it in the location noted below and open the tab for the month you're looking at.

2

Find an open date

Each weekday has a box underneath it. If it's blank, the shift is open.

3

Type your first name

After you hit enter in the cell, it will be saved. You can close the file when done as auto-save is on.
