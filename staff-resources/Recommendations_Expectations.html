How to chart a form in Recommendations

  1. Open the Recommendations component.
  2. Click the row of the expectation you are documenting.
  3. When the side panel opens, click the Actions button.
  4. Click the form in the list (usually the last option) to chart.

Other Recommendations rules

  • Hepatitis C screening: the recommendation rule qualifies patients 18 to 79.
  • Annual Wellness Visit: focused on patients 19 and older.
  • Initial Preventive Physical Examination: a one-time exam for Medicare patients, focused on patients 65 and older.
  • Adult Wellness Exam (Female and Male): the order "Annual wellness visit; Initial visit, including a personalized prevention plan of service (PPPS)" satisfies this expectation.
