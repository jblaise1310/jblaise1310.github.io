⚙ Pre-Visit Order Workflow

1 Provider — Writes referral order and adds comments noting any pending labs, imaging, or screenings required before the specialist visit.
2 Referral Specialist — Reviews comments and sets status to Prep for send / Additional information needed if pending orders are present.
3 PSR — Monitors referrals with the “Additional information needed” substatus daily. Attaches results when available, removes substatus, and reassigns to Vickie Perales.
4 Vickie Perales — Reviews for accuracy and completeness, then sends. If documents are incomplete, adds substatus back and unassigns — returns to PSR queue.
