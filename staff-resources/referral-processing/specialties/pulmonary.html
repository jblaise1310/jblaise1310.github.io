ER / Hospital Discharge Follow-Up Timing

⏱ Standard Follow-Up Window

Per Dr. Holcomb, the standard of care is for patients to follow up with the Pulmonary office 2 to 4 weeks after an ER visit or hospitalization.

  • Follow-up sooner than 2 weeks is generally not necessary and does not affect the patient’s plan of care.
  • When scheduling post-discharge referrals, target the 2–4 week window.

Operational Notes

📋 PFT Availability

Patients referred for COPD or Pulmonary Hypertension sometimes struggle to complete PFT quickly due to scheduling availability. Dr. Holcomb has coordinated with Vicki and the RTs to increase PFT capacity. If a patient is unable to complete PFT before the referral appointment window, contact the specialty team to discuss alternatives.

📨 Incomplete Referral Response

When a referral is received without required testing, a standard notification is sent back to the referring provider. A formal return form is in development. Until then, contact the specialty team directly if you receive an incomplete referral rejection.
