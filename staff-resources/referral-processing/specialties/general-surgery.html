Common Referral Reasons

📋 Typical Reasons for Referral

  • Hernia repair evaluation (inguinal, umbilical, ventral)
  • Gallbladder disease / cholecystitis
  • Appendicitis follow-up
  • Skin or soft tissue mass or lesion
  • Colorectal evaluation
  • Abdominal pain of surgical concern
  • Post-bariatric follow-up

Referral Requirements

⚠ Patient Eligibility

BMI must be 40 or below for a consultation referral to be accepted. Confirm the patient's BMI before submitting.

📄 Required with Every Referral

  • Clinical / office visit notes
  • Previous colonoscopy report if available
  • Previous imaging reports if available

Commonly Attached Documents

📄 Documents to Gather

  • Office visit note
  • Lab results
  • Imaging reports if available

Processing Notes

✎ Notes & Reminders

  • Internal referrals to Dr. Gazenko have a separate worklist: Dr. Gazenko handles Review and Process; RN Gen Surg handles scheduling and completion
  • External General Surgery referrals are managed by Referral Specialist with discharge scheduling
  • Colonoscopy referrals follow a similar path but process through Dr. Tinguely — verify current assignment
  • Include imaging study details and dates in referral comments when available
