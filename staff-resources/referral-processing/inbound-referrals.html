Referral Specialist Workflow

Inbound Referrals

For referrals received from outside providers to Primary Care Clinic. Inbound referral faxes are found in the Specialist Referrals Fax Folder on the Z: Shared Drive.

Where to find inbound referrals: External referrals from outside providers are faxed to us and appear in the Specialist Referrals Fax Folder on the Z: Shared Drive. Work from this folder to complete each step below.
1
Search for the Patient in PowerChart
Determine if the patient is already established at PSMC

From the taskbar, click Patient then click Search. Enter the patient's date of birth (DOB) to search.

PowerChart Taskbar
Click Patient in the taskbar, then Search. Enter the patient's DOB.
▼ Based on your search result, follow the correct path:
✓ Patient Found (Established)

Open the patient's chart by selecting an encounter and clicking OK. Select Chart Review and click OK.

Then scan the referral into the chart:

  • Click Patient from the taskbar → View Images
  • Click NEW at the bottom
  • Image Type = Provider Orders
  • Date of Referral = date faxed to us
  • Image Title = (Specialty) Referral — e.g., Orthopedic Referral or Dr. Webb Referral
  • Click Import → find referral in fax folder → double-click to attach
  • Click Sign and close the box

Referral is now scanned in. Proceed to Step 3.

⚠ Patient Not Found (New)

The patient will need a new profile built before you can scan in the referral.

Proceed to Step 2 to build the patient profile first.

Note: You will not be able to scan the referral into the chart until after the referral is built for new patients.

2
Build a New Patient Profile
New patients only — skip to Step 3 if the patient already exists

From the taskbar, click PM Conversation and choose Add/Modify Person.

PM Conversation Menu
Click PM Conversation in the taskbar, then Add/Modify Person.

Complete all required fields (highlighted in yellow), then:

  • Scroll down in the Patient Information tab to select a Primary Care Provider (not yellow but required)
  • Add primary and secondary insurance information
  • Select the appropriate Patient Relationship to Subscriber (generally Self)
  • Select Employment Status if applicable or required
  • Click Search for Health Plan → search By Plan Name
  • Set Verified Status = Pending
Insurance shortcuts: Medicare = MCR  |  Medicaid = MCD  |  Blue Cross Blue Shield = BCBS  |  Self Pay = SELF PAY. If unsure which plan is correct, select your best guess — it will be verified and updated at Registration.

Click Complete when done. Then return to Step 1 to scan the referral into the patient's newly created chart before proceeding to Step 3.

3
Build the Referral in Referral Management
Complete Case Details for the inbound referral

From the Referral Management screen, click Add Inbound Referral in the top right corner. Enter the patient's DOB to find them, select the patient, then click OK.

Add Inbound Referral Button
Use the Add Inbound Referral button in the top right of the RM screen.

If this is an existing patient, add the scanned referral document from the Documents field. Skip this if it was a new patient build.

Complete the Case Details fields:

  • Medical Service: Select RM (Specialty) ⚠ Known Glitch — you will need to do this step twice due to a system glitch
  • Referral Reason: Found on the referral document
  • Codified Reason: Enter the ICD-10 code and select the data
  • Referred From: Outside provider — most often not in the system; leave blank or use the magnifying glass to search by last name
  • Referral Written Date: Date the referral was faxed to us
  • Refer To: Click the magnifying glass, find the provider, and select
  • Priority: Routine unless otherwise specified on the referral
  • Instructions to Staff: Use for any pertinent routing information — e.g., "Referrals from Jicarilla (Dulce Clinic) request a phone call from Registration once patient is scheduled"
  • Comments: If the referring provider is not found in the system, add their name, facility, phone number, and fax number here
Known system glitch: After completing Case Details, click Next at the bottom right, then click Previous, then click Next again. This is required to advance the referral correctly.

Review the Referral Details. If everything looks correct, click Submit. If corrections are needed, click Previous, make edits, then return to review and submit.

4
Scheduling / Attach an Appointment
Check for an existing appointment and attach if found
  • Go to the Provider Process screen
  • Find the patient you just built the referral for and open the referral
  • Go to the Scheduling tab
  • Check whether the patient already has an appointment scheduled for this specialty
▼ Does the patient have an existing appointment?
✓ Appointment Exists

Attach the appointment to the referral. Status will change to Scheduled.

─ No Appointment Yet

Leave the status as-is. The referral remains in queue for scheduling.

5
Finalize the Referral
Final steps vary by patient type and specialty
Existing Patient — These specialties: Neurology, Pulmonology, General Surgery / Dr. Gazenko

If the patient was already established, the referral was already scanned into their chart in Step 1, and you have submitted the referral in Step 3. You are done.

Existing Patient — Orthopedics
  • Go to the Provider Process screen and find the patient
  • Status will show Pending Acceptance
  • Open the referral and click Accept in the top right
  • In the yellow dropdown, select Preadmit
  • Click Edit under where it says “Accepted”
  • Click Call Patient to Schedule
  • You are done.
New Patient — All Specialties

You still need to scan the referral into the chart (it could not be done before the profile was built). Follow these steps:

  • Go to the Provider Process tab and open the referral
  • Click Accept in the top right, then click Pre-Admit
  • Close out of the referral and click on the patient's name
  • Click Chart Review
  • Scan the referral into the chart using the same steps as Step 1 (Patient → View Images → New → Provider Orders, etc.)
Preadmit Encounter Dialog
Set Type to Preadmit and Location to PSMC Clinic, then click Submit.
  • Go back to the Provider Process screen and open the referral
  • Change the status from Accepted back to Pending Acceptance and click Submit
  • Click on the Documents tab in the referral and add the scanned referral document
  • Close the referral
  • Delete the inbound referral document from the Specialist Referral Folder on the Z: Drive
  • You are done.

Quick Reference

✍ Inbound Referral Checklist

  • Locate referral in Specialist Referrals Fax Folder on Z: Drive
  • Step 1: Search patient by DOB in PowerChart (Patient → Search)
  • If found: Scan referral into chart (Patient → View Images → New → Provider Orders) → go to Step 3
  • If not found: Build patient profile in Step 2 first, then return to scan after referral is built
  • Step 2 (new patients): PM Conversation → Add/Modify Person → complete required fields including PCP and insurance
  • Step 3: Add Inbound Referral in RM → enter DOB → complete Case Details → Next → Previous → Next (glitch workaround) → Submit
  • Step 4: Provider Process → check Scheduling tab → attach existing appointment if found
  • Step 5: Finalize per specialty / patient type (see table above)
  • Last step for new patients: Delete referral from Z: Drive Specialist Referral Folder
Known system glitch reminder: When building a new referral in Step 3, after completing Case Details you must click Next → Previous → Next to advance correctly. Skipping this will cause issues with submission.
