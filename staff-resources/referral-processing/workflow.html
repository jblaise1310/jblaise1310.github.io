Referral Processing Workflow

Referral management moves through three defined stages: Review, Process, and Schedule Tracking. Each stage has assigned worklist managers and defined referral statuses. Use this page as your guide to how referrals move from order to completion.

Note: The worklist assignments on this page reflect an earlier version of our referral guide and may not reflect current staff assignments or processes. Use this as a reference framework and confirm current assignments with your supervisor. This page will be updated as processes are finalized.

The Three Stages of Referral Processing

1
First Step
Review

Worklist manager gathers and reviews all documents to attach to the referral. Referral moves from Not Started into active review.

Statuses at this stage
  • Not StartedNew order has arrived on the referral worklist
  • Prep for SendWorklist manager has begun processing; documents being gathered
  • CancelledUsed for duplicates, or when referred-to provider is unavailable and a new order is needed
›
2
Second Step
Process

Worklist manager accepts or rejects the referral as appropriate. Rejected referrals are handled by the processing provider (Dr. Tinguely, Pain Clinic, or Marcia).

Statuses at this stage
  • AcceptedReferral accepted and ready for scheduling
  • Pending AcceptanceReferral is in the Process worklist but not yet accepted or rejected
  • Pending ReschedulePatient no-showed or cancelled without rescheduling. Scheduler will attempt contact 3 times before cancelling.
  • RejectedReferral rejected — seen by processing provider
  • HoldPlaced on hold by processor, awaiting additional information
›
3
Third Step
Schedule Tracking

Split into two sub-steps: scheduling the patient (3A) and completing the referral after the visit (3B).

3A — Schedule
  • ScheduledAppointment is associated and scheduled
3B — Complete
  • Patient SeenAuto-set when patient checks in to their appointment
  • CompletedSet by worklist manager once Patient Seen status appears. Removes referral from the worklist.

Referral Status Quick Reference

Where to view referral status: Turn on the Referrals Component on your mPage. Click the hamburger icon and select Referrals. Referrals display under In-Progress or Historical filters — check both if you can’t find a record. Click the row to open the side panel.

Pre-Visit Order Workflow

When a provider orders labs, imaging, or screenings that must be completed before the specialist visit, the referral follows this additional workflow within the Review stage.
1
Provider
Write referral order and add comments
Write the referral order in the system. If pending orders (labs, imaging, screenings) must be completed before the specialist visit, add a comment to the referral listing which orders were placed. If no pending orders, the referral proceeds through the standard workflow.
2
Referral Specialist
Review comments and set status
Review the referral and read the provider’s comments. If pending orders are noted, set the referral status:
  • Status: Prep for send
  • Substatus: Additional information needed
This substatus is the trigger for the PSR to begin monitoring. If no pending orders are noted, process and send per the usual workflow.
3
PSR (Admin)
Monitor hold queue daily
Monitor all referrals with substatus Additional information needed. Check daily whether the orders noted in the provider’s comments have resulted. When all orders are complete:
  • Attach results to the referral
  • Remove the Additional information needed substatus
  • Reassign the referral to Vickie Perales
4
Vickie Perales — Referral Specialist
Final review and send
Review the referral for accuracy and completeness. If all documents are correct, send the referral and continue processing. If an issue is found:
  • Add Additional information needed substatus back
  • Unassign yourself — referral returns to the PSR queue
  • Loop continues until referral is accurate and ready to send

Viewing Referrals on Your mPage

⚙ Enable the Referrals Component

  • Click the hamburger icon on your mPage and select Referrals
  • Once loaded, referrals display under In-Progress or Historical filters
  • Check both filters if you cannot locate a specific record
  • Click any row to open the side panel and view referral details, status, sub-statuses, and attached documents

🔍 Finding a Referral with "Refer To" Binoculars

When placing a referral order, use the binoculars icon next to the Refer To field to search for and select the correct receiving provider or team. Selecting the wrong destination is one of the most common causes of referral delays.
