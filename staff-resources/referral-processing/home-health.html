Home Health Referrals

How to confirm a patient qualifies for home health, send the referral, and complete the Home Health Certification and Face to Face Encounter form.

Services a Provider Can Refer For

Physical Therapy Occupational Therapy Skilled Nursing Speech Therapy

Home Health Qualifications

The patient must meet all of these

  • Certified homebound: unable to leave the residence due to their condition, or unable to ambulate.
  • The same services are not being provided elsewhere (for example, outpatient PT or OT).
  • The reason for referral must pass Medicare guidelines for diagnosis codes and medical conditions.
  • Clinical findings support the need for skilled nursing and physical therapy.
  • The patient was seen face to face in the past 90 days for a condition relevant to the home health referral.
  • The patient has a provider for follow-up care who can sign orders.

Qualifying and Non-Qualifying Conditions

Qualifying conditions

  • Post-surgical recovery, complex wound care, rehab after joint replacement
  • Cardiovascular disease, acute or chronic heart failure
  • Pulmonary disorders, severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
  • Neurological incidents, stroke, dementia
  • Diabetes

Non-qualifying conditions

  • Frail elderly
  • Weakness
  • Deconditioning
  • Fatigue

Services Not Covered by Home Health

  • Housekeeping
  • Meal preparation
  • 24 hour daily care
  • Delivery or errands
  • Personal care, like dressing

Step by Step

Part 1: Send the Home Health Care referral
1
Confirm the qualifying need
Check the patient against the qualifications and conditions above.
2
Send the referral with the note or notes
Include the office visit note from the last 90 days.
3
Wait for Home Health to respond
Home Health will advise whether the patient is accepted for care.
Part 2: Fill out the Home Health Certification and Face to Face Encounter form
4
Enter patient and provider information
Patient demographics, including the last 4 of the social security number. Add the primary care provider, and the ordering provider if different.
5
Enter the encounter
Encounter date and reason, taken from the referral and the office visit note.
6
Check the services needed
Check the box for each service the patient needs.
7
Complete the clinical sections
Evaluate and treat: choose the relevant items from Potential Examples #1. Clinical findings that support status: choose from Potential Examples #2.
8
Date the form and send it to the provider to sign
9
Scan the signed Face to Face into the patient’s chart
10
Send the signed Face to Face to Home Health
11
Referral completed and closed
Potential Examples #1 and #2 to be added Step 7 refers to two lists of example wording (for evaluate and treat, and for clinical findings). Those lists were not included in the source document. Send them over and they can be added here.

Quick Reference Checklist

✍ Home Health Checklist

  • Patient is homebound and has been seen face to face in the last 90 days
  • Condition qualifies (not frail elderly, weakness, deconditioning, or fatigue)
  • Send the referral with the office visit note
  • After Home Health accepts: complete the Certification and Face to Face form
  • Provider signs, then scan into the chart
  • Send the signed Face to Face to Home Health and close the referral
