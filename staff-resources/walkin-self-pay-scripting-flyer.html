RHC self-pay walk-in encounters

Scripting for Collection on RHC Encounters (Self-Pay)

Hello, Good Morning, etc. (Patient's Name/Guarantor Name)

Example: "Ms. Campbell, we are asking for a payment of $100.00 toward your visit today, and the final charge(s) will be based on the services provided to you today. You will receive a statement from Pagosa Springs Medical Center within 10 days from the date of service."

"If you pay in full within 30 days of your first statement date, you will receive a 30% discount off of your total charges for this visit today. We accept American Express, Discover, MasterCard, Visa, checks and cash. Do you have any questions?"

If They Can't Pay

Suggest referring to our Financial Counselor for assistance, after they see the provider. They can also stop by and see the Financial Counselor directly.

Common Errors to Avoid

  • Telling the patient $100 is the "full cost" of the visit.
  • Skipping the explanation of the 10-day statement timeline.
  • Forgetting to mention the 30% prompt-pay discount.
  • Offering the Financial Counselor referral before the patient has seen the provider.
