Module 6: Message Center & Artera Management

Two systems, one job: make sure nothing sent to this clinic goes unanswered.

A message that sits unread isn't just a missed task, it's a patient or provider waiting on the other end of it. These two systems are where that waiting happens if you fall behind.

Cerner Message Center

The internal inbox where clinical messages, orders, and results get routed to the right person.

  • Routes messages, orders, and results to the correct pool or person, so nothing sits unseen
  • Your role is accurate routing, not clinical interpretation, getting a message to the right person quickly matters more than understanding its content
  • A message that sits too long is a delay for a provider or a patient, treat this inbox with the same urgency as a ringing phone

Artera Patient Messaging

Two-way texting with patients, often their primary line of contact with the clinic outside a phone call.

  • Sends automated reminders, but patients can and do text back, and those replies need a real person watching for them
  • For some patients, this is easier and more comfortable than a phone call, treat it with the same care as Module 2's phone presence guidance
  • Monitor it at the start and end of your shift and periodically throughout the day, same expectation as the Message Center inbox
