A Note on Scope

These reminders are about gathering information, prepping the room, and flagging what you notice, checking a chart date, exposing an area, confirming a symptom timeline. Ordering labs, reconciling medications, and interpreting results stay with the provider. When in doubt, note exactly what the patient told you and let the provider take it from there.
