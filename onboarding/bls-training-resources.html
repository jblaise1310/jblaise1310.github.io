Employee Onboarding

Welcome to Your Training Resources

Employees can now register for all American Heart Association (AHA) certification courses directly through HealthStream. This updated process helps streamline onboarding, training enrollment, and certification management for all staff.

Basic Life Support (BLS) certification is required for all employees.

If you are unsure which certifications are required for your role, please contact your manager.

New HealthStream registration process

Employees may now register for all AHA certification classes directly in HealthStream, including:

BLS — Basic Life Support ACLS — Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support PALS — Pediatric Advanced Life Support

This new process eliminates the need for email back-and-forth to schedule classes.

Already registered for a class?

No action is needed. Existing registrations have been manually enrolled and should appear in your HealthStream "To Do" tasks.

Log in to confirm your enrollment is visible under your assigned tasks.

How to register

1
Log into HealthStream Training using the desktop icon.
2
Select the Catalog tab.
3
Search for one of the following courses:
BLS ACLS PALS
4
Select the Info tab to review course details and learning objectives.
5
Click Choose Class or Enroll to select a class date and time.

Need to reschedule? Use View Class in HealthStream to reschedule, withdraw, or drop a course.
