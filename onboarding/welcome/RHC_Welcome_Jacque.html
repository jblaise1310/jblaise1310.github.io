First-Day Arrival

A relaxed start for day one
Morning huddle, every day at 7:00 AM. We gather as a team each morning to walk through the day's schedule and any operations items, so everyone starts on the same page. For your first day you came in at 8:00 so we could get you settled; going forward, plan to arrive a few minutes before the 7:00 huddle.
What to wear: Scrubs in any color you like, with closed-toe, non-slip shoes.
Getting you set up: As a new team member, your first day includes getting your photo ID badge made and your system access started (Cerner and your other logins). Your preceptors and I will walk you through where to go and who to see, so you don't need to sort any of it out on your own.

You won't be finding your way alone. Three experienced team members will be precepting you as you get started, each bringing a different vantage point on the work.
