Rural Health Clinic at PSMC — Field Notes07 · The Team Model
How Care Is Organized

The Team
Model

Care here isn't delivered by individuals working alone — it's delivered by teams. Here's how a team is built, and how each role relates to the others.

MD

Physician

Leads the team's clinical scope and manages their own patient panel. A resource for the team's mid-levels on complex or unusual cases.

PA/FNP

Mid-Level Provider (PA / FNP-C)

Manages their own patient panel independently — not the physician's patients. Collaborates with the physician as a peer, not a subordinate.

RN

Registered Nurse

Shared across the whole team — triage, nursing care, and coordination for every provider's patients, not just one.

MA

Medical Assistant

Paired one-to-one with a single provider — rooming, vitals, and chart prep for that provider's patients specifically.

PSR

Patient Service Representative

The team's front line — scheduling, check-in/out, and phones for every patient the team sees.

Shared Across the Team

These roles support every provider on the team, not just one.

Registered Nurse1 per team
Patient Service Rep1 per team
Paired to One Provider

These belong to a single provider, not the whole team.

Medical Assistant1 per provider
Patient PanelOwned individually

Same structure, repeated three times: Red Team, Blue Team, and Green Team each run this exact model, independently.

!

One Team, Separate Patient Panels

Teams share a workspace, an RN, and a PSR — and lean on each other for coverage, curbside questions, and complex cases. But each provider, physician or mid-level, manages their own patient panel. Mid-level providers see their own patients; they are not managing the physician's patients on the team. Collaboration is built in — patient assignment is not shared.

Specialty services (Pulmonary, Orthopedics, Neurology, Cardiology) run their own structures based on the specialty — this model describes how the three primary care teams are built.

Clinical Leadership

The Medical
Director

Every team and service line eventually has a clinical question that needs to go up the chain. Here's who that is, and what the role covers.

RS

Dr. Ryan Stopher-Mitchell

Medical Director

Provides clinical oversight for the entire clinic — not just Red, Blue, and Green — including every specialty service line. The clinic's top point of reference for medical policy and quality of care.

01

Sets Clinical Standards

Establishes and maintains the protocols, guidelines, and quality benchmarks every team and service line follows.

02

Clinical Escalation Point

The go-to for complex cases, unusual presentations, or clinical questions that go beyond day-to-day scope, clinic-wide.

03

Oversees Scope & Credentialing

Ensures every provider is practicing within their credentialed scope, and supports credentialing as providers join or grow.

04

Quality & Safety

Leads clinical quality review and safety initiatives, translating outcomes data into changes teams can act on.

Rural Health Clinic at PSMC — Field Notes11 · Team, Meet Teams
Stay Connected

Team, Meet
Teams

Microsoft Teams is how we stay in sync — real-time updates, shared documents, and alignment across every role in the clinic. It's a required tool for all RHC staff, used daily to communicate clinic updates and keep departments aligned.

Daily Expectations

All staff are expected to check the All Staff channel every day.

  • This is where important updates, daily reminders, and announcements are posted.
  • Posts may include workflow changes, staffing alerts, safety messages, quality updates, or team highlights.
  • Skipping this step can mean missing information that affects patient care and clinic operations.
How We Use Teams, Professionally
01Use your full name when messaging coworkers in shared spaces.
02Keep messages brief, respectful, and work-focused.
03Post in the correct channel to ensure visibility and reduce confusion.
04Avoid 1:1 chats for task assignments when coverage or visibility is needed — post in the shared Team or Channel instead.
05Use @mention tags to notify the right person or group (e.g., @Jo Blaise, @CMA Team).

Microsoft Teams is a key part of how we operate as a team — treat it as required reading, not a side channel.

Field Guide, Part 1

Speak the
Language

Clinical roles — the abbreviations you'll see on schedules, charts, and team assignments.

MD /
DO

Physician

The clinic's doctors. Diagnose and treat patients, and lead each care team's medical decisions.

PA

Physician Assistant

Licensed to examine, diagnose, treat, and prescribe, working in collaboration with a physician.

FNP-C

Family Nurse Practitioner, Certified

An advanced-practice nurse who can diagnose, treat, and prescribe, often as a primary provider.

RN

Registered Nurse

Provides direct patient care, triage, and medication administration, and coordinates the care team.
