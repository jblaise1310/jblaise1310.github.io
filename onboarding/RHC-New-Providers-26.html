Joining Our Team

We are proud to welcome four exceptional providers to PSMC this fall. Please join us in making them feel right at home.

👨‍⚕️
Primary Care · Family Medicine
Dr. George Norwood
📅 Joining September 14, 2026

Dr. Norwood is completing his Family Medicine Residency at the University of Arizona in Tucson, where he has been an active member of the Quality Improvement Committee and has contributed to research in areas including opiate use disorder management and shoulder dystocia safety. He is board eligible in Family Medicine and brings a strong foundation in outpatient care, quality improvement, and clinical research. We are so excited to have him joining our primary care team.

🏥 Family Medicine 🎓 University of Arizona ✅ Board Eligible 📊 Quality Improvement 💻 Cerner EHR
🧠
Neurology · Neurocritical Care
Dr. Arturo Montaño
📅 Joining September 15, 2026

Dr. Montaño brings an exceptional depth of expertise in neurology to PSMC. He completed his neurology residency and a Neurocritical Care fellowship at the University of California San Francisco, and most recently has served as Division Chief of Neurocritical Care and Stroke Co-Medical Director at Denver Health. His arrival marks a significant expansion of our neurology capabilities and will bring specialized care closer to home for our community.

🧠 Neurology 🏥 UCSF Fellowship ⭐ Denver Health Division Chief 🩺 Neurocritical Care 🫀 Stroke Co-Medical Director

Primary Care · Family Medicine
Dr. Chad Gray
📅 Joining Full Time November 1, 2026

Many of you already know Dr. Gray well. He has been covering for us in both the clinic and the Emergency Department for years, and has become a familiar and trusted face to our team and our patients. We are so excited to share that he will be joining PSMC as a full-time Primary Care Provider beginning November 1st. This is wonderful news, and we know the whole team will be thrilled to have him here permanently!

🏥 Primary Care 🤝 Known to Our Team 📍 Full Time
👩‍⚕️
Primary Care · Family Medicine
Dr. Erin Renfrew
📅 Joining November 9, 2026

Dr. Renfrew is a board-certified Family Medicine physician currently practicing at the Northern Navajo Medical Center, where she provides a broad scope of care including inpatient, outpatient, ICU, and obstetrics in a rural underserved setting. A Colorado State University graduate, she holds licensure in both Colorado and New Mexico. Dr. Renfrew's deep commitment to rural medicine and her comprehensive clinical background make her an exceptional fit for our team and our community.
