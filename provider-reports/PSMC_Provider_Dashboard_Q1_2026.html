Director analysis — Q1 2026
✓ Doing well
Scheduled PC visit volume is strong. RSM (212/mo) and McIntyre (213/mo) lead volume. Nobles at 14.4 vpd and RSM at 12.8 vpd are both above the group average despite very different panel sizes and schedule structures.
Three new providers actively ramping. Hintzman panel +45% in 4 months (108→157). Johnson +24% (152→188). Barber starts July. Healthy pipeline.
Steed surgical volume grew 87% YTD — 43 cases vs 23 all of 2025. In only his second year. Positive, engaged, wants more volume.
Liverett zero no-shows across all four months. Walk-in model at work — VPD of 13.4 avg is notably high for walk-in structure.
Holcomb and Steed maintain near-zero after-hours EHR. 0% and 0.8% respectively. Protected work-life separation.
⚠ Monitor closely
Nobles after-hours reached 21.8% in April — highest in the dataset. Averaging 18.1% for Q1 on a part-time 8-day/month schedule.
Zappone no-shows spiked to 15 in April — Q1 total of 36 is highest of any provider. Pattern erratic (9, 4, 8, 15).
Mashburn message center at 21.4% avg — sustained from 2025. Returns from maternity leave July. Review before her first back interview.
Webb surgical volume declining sharply. 18 YTD vs 37 full-year 2025 (−51%). Referral pipeline or OR access issue?
Johnson EHR doc time 16–24 min/patient. Expected for new provider — should decrease through Q2.
✗ Genuine concern — action required
Access crisis across scheduled primary care. RSM booked to Aug 3 (74 days), McIntyre Jul 15 (55 days), Mashburn Jul 28 (68 days). Five providers with waits exceeding 3 weeks simultaneously is systemic.
Nobles NP slot not until November 16 — 179 days out. No new patient NP slot for 6 months. Most acute access gap in the practice.
RSM panel declining while booked 74 days out. Panel dropped 32 patients (−2.2%) while inaccessible to new patients. Likely attrition.
McIntyre March compression event root cause unknown. 21.6% after-hours, 17 visits/day in 10 days. Panel forecast ~900 by September without intervention.
