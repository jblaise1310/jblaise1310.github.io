When to escalate

Non-RN roles escalate. RNs triage within approved protocols.

Question about a reading, symptom or treatment
Coordinator or Navigator routes it to the patient’s RN. RPM questions go to the RN PHCC.
Urgent symptom or abnormal finding
RN triages under approved protocols and notifies the provider.
Safety, abuse, neglect or crisis concern
Escalate right away under policy. Do not wait for report-back.
Emergency
Call 911, then notify the patient’s RN and provider.
Data or system problem
Report to the Analyst or Informatics. Do not change clinical data to fix a list.

Ground rules

What keeps the team from doubling up.

  1. One accountable nurse. The RN PHCC holds every patient she routes. Everyone else is a service lead who reports back.
  2. The right first call. The RN PHCC makes a short intake call and names the referral. Each service lead then makes their own first call.
  3. One outreach log. Every contact attempt goes in the same place in the EHR, so no patient gets several calls for the same reason.
  4. Patients can change their minds. A patient who opts into CCM later moves to the RN Care Manager, and the RN PHCC is notified.
  5. One funded category. A patient can count toward more than one program, but staff time is charged to only one.
