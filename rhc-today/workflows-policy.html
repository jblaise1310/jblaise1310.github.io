Timecard & Payroll

⏱️
Every Pay Period — No Exceptions

Timecard Review & Approval

Every employee must review and approve their timecard each pay period and confirm their paycheck in Paycom.

  • ✅Did my hours look right?
  • ✅Did I request all my time off in Paycom?
  • ✅Does my paycheck reflect what I expected?
ℹ️

Managers cannot add PTO or sick leave on your behalf. All requests must be submitted directly by you in Paycom.

Updated Routing Process

IT Support Requests — New Flow

To prevent duplicate tickets and get problems resolved faster, please follow the updated process:

  1. Password resets — continue through the usual self-service process. No change here.
  2. Everything else (printers, desktop setup, device issues, software) — contact Vicki or Jo first before submitting to IT.

This flow prevents duplicate tickets, ensures the right details are included the first time, and helps us prioritize clinic needs as a whole.

Patient Scheduling & Access

🕐
Active Change

Arrival Time vs. Appointment Time

All patient appointment reminders now display the arrival time, not the appointment time. Staff must communicate arrival time at the point of booking, tailored by appointment type.

💡

This change is designed to reduce registration bottlenecks and provider delays. Please ensure all scheduling staff are aware.

📋
Active Process

Patient Waitlist — Primary Care

The Primary Care Clinic now uses a centralized waitlist for patients who cannot be scheduled immediately.

  • 🩺RHC nurses triage for urgent needs first
  • 📝Non-urgent patients are added to the waitlist
  • 📞Patients are contacted in order of request date
💬

Direct questions about the waitlist to clinic leadership.

Facility Protocol

Mouse Droppings — Cleanup Protocol

If you find evidence of mice, follow the CDC-recommended protocol. Do not sweep or vacuum droppings.

  • 🧤Wear surgical mask (purple or yellow) and gloves
  • 🧴Spray bleach-based cleaner on droppings — soak for at least 5 minutes
  • 🗑️Wipe up with bleach wipe or paper towel, place in plastic bag, seal, and dispose
  • 🙌Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water
  • 🪑If carpet is affected, follow the above then submit a facilities ticket for assessment
ℹ️

Facilities has activated Orkin and traps are in place. Keep all food in sealed containers at all times — desks, drawers, break rooms, and offices.
