Inclement Weather Guidance

❄️

Inclement Weather — Closures & Staffing Expectations

During inclement weather events, follow these steps to stay informed and plan accordingly:

  • 📧Check your PSMC email first — official closure and delay notices are sent there
  • 💬Monitor Teams for real-time updates from leadership
  • 📞If you are unable to make it in safely, contact your supervisor as early as possible
  • 🏥Assume the clinic is open unless you receive an official notice — do not assume closure
  • 🚗Allow extra travel time during weather events and communicate proactively with your team
💡 Tip: Save the Facilities after-hours line (970-507-3900) in your phone for building access questions during weather emergencies.
