📅 Fun Facts About August

Named after Roman Emperor Augustus — originally the sixth month, "Sextilis," before Julius Caesar's calendar reform
August is the only month with no official U.S. federal holiday
National Aviation Day falls on August 19 — Orville Wright's birthday
The "Dog Days of Summer" get their name from Sirius, the "Dog Star," prominent in the August sky in ancient times
Colorado's summer monsoon season peaks in August — expect those classic afternoon thunderstorms here in Pagosa
Beat the Heat, Stay Hydrated
You First: A Monthly Wellness Pause
August brings some of the warmest, driest days of the year here in Pagosa — and at 7,000+ feet, dehydration can sneak up faster than it would at lower elevations. A few small habits go a long way toward feeling steady through the workday.
This month's tip: build hydration into your shift, not just your breaks.
  • Keep a water bottle at your station — refill it each time you pass the break room
  • Pair caffeine with a glass of water, since coffee alone won't cut it up here
  • Notice headaches or afternoon fatigue — often the first sign you're behind on fluids, not just tired
  • A pinch of electrolytes (or a piece of fruit) helps on long, active days
This isn't medical advice — just a friendly nudge. If you're managing a health condition where fluid intake matters, follow your own provider's guidance first.
