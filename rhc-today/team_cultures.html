Sleep Hygiene
You First: A Monthly Wellness Pause
Healthcare workers are among the most sleep-deprived professionals — and poor sleep affects focus, mood, and patient care. The good news? Small, consistent habits can make a real difference.
Simple tips for better rest:
  • Keep a consistent sleep and wake time — even on weekends
  • Avoid screens for 30–60 minutes before bed
  • Keep your bedroom cool, dark, and quiet
  • Limit caffeine after 2:00 PM
  • Try a short wind-down routine — even 10 minutes of reading or stretching helps signal your brain it's time to rest
Quality sleep supports your immune system, mental health, and the energy you bring to your patients and your team. You deserve good rest. Thank you for all you do.
