RUTH VANCE
LPN/LVN: Clinic
"Ruth has been part of this clinic since 1983, when she joined the original Mary Fisher Clinic looking for a supportive place to raise her family. She's watched this community's healthcare grow and change ever since. Outside of work she finds joy in gardening, hiking with friends, and crocheting and sewing when her hands allow it. What keeps her energized day to day is the camaraderie with her younger coworkers ('they crack me up!') and patient care that still feels deeply fulfilling after four decades. She leans on the Head Nurse for technical questions and goes straight to the Clinic Manager for anything more sensitive. Given the chance, Ruth would love to shadow other departments or put her unused LPN skills (including her IV certification) back to work, craving the broader clinical exposure that rural practice doesn't always allow."

🍂 September: Did You Know?

September may be the 9th month of the year, but its name comes from the Latin word septem, meaning seven. It was originally the seventh month of the ancient Roman calendar!

🌕 The full moon closest to the fall equinox is traditionally called the Harvest Moon.
🌾 September has long been associated with harvest season, which is why apples, pumpkins, wheat, and changing leaves are classic symbols of the month.
📅 September has 30 days: "Thirty days hath September…"
