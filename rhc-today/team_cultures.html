Sun Safety at 7,000 Feet
You First: A Monthly Wellness Pause
Living and working in Pagosa Springs means we're at a higher elevation than most — and that means UV exposure is significantly stronger here than at sea level. Summer sun can feel wonderful, but unprotected exposure adds up fast.
Simple habits to protect yourself this summer:
  • Apply SPF 30+ sunscreen every morning — even on cloudy days
  • Reapply every 2 hours when outdoors, especially near water
  • Wear a hat and UV-protective sunglasses when outside
  • Seek shade during peak hours — 10 AM to 4 PM
  • Stay hydrated — dehydration and sun exposure are a tough combination
  • Check your skin regularly and schedule an annual skin exam
You spend your days taking care of others — take a few extra minutes to take care of yourself too. You are worth protecting.

Wellness Program Reminder: If you carry PSMC insurance, you have access to the Power of Vitality program — earn points, set personal wellness goals, and track progress toward rewards.
