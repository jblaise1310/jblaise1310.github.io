What's changing today
Workflow 1
In-Office BP Averaging

If the first reading is <140/90 — chart it. If it's >140/90 — write it down, take a second, and chart the lower systolic and lower diastolic from either reading.

Workflow 2
Home BP Averaging

Use the most recent 2–7 days of home readings (per provider instruction), exclude Day 1, average the rest, and document on the Blood Pressure AdHoc form in Cerner.
