🕯️ A Halloween Story
The Haunting of Exam Room Three

It started on a quiet Tuesday in October, the kind of morning where the mountains wore clouds like hats and the waiting room smelled of coffee and hand sanitizer. Room Three had always been a little odd. The blood pressure cuff inflated on its own sometimes. The paper on the exam table crinkled when no one was sitting on it. And once, the otoscope light flickered three times in the middle of a perfectly normal ear check.

🎃 👻 🕷️

The staff had a theory. Old Dr. Wellby, who had practiced in the building for thirty years before retiring to Arizona, had left something behind. Not anything frightening, mind you. Just his stethoscope, which had somehow ended up in the third drawer of the supply cart, and his habit of humming softly while charting. Sometimes, late in the afternoon when the clinic grew quiet, you could almost hear it.

It was Renee who first noticed that patients in Room Three always seemed to leave a little calmer than when they arrived. Blood pressures that had been elevated in triage read perfectly normal by the time the provider walked in. Anxious patients smiled. Fussy toddlers stopped fussing. "Room Three has good energy," she told anyone who would listen.

🍂 🕸️ 🌙

So this Halloween, when you walk past Room Three and the paper crinkles for no reason at all, do not be alarmed. That is just Dr. Wellby, making sure the thermostat is set right and the exam light is angled correctly, the way he always did. Some things, it turns out, do not haunt. They simply stay because they love the work.

Happy Halloween from your RHC team. 🎃

🍺 Prost! It's Oktoberfest!
Oktoberfest began in Munich, Germany in 1810 as a celebration of the royal wedding of Crown Prince Ludwig and Princess Therese. What started as a five-day horse race became the world's largest folk festival, drawing over six million visitors each year. The spirit of Oktoberfest is really about community, good food, laughter, and celebrating the season together. Sounds a lot like us!

🎃 October: Did You Know?

October is the tenth month of the year, but like September, its name comes from the Latin word "octo," meaning eight — a leftover from the ancient Roman calendar when the year started in March.

🎃 The tradition of carving jack-o'-lanterns originated in Ireland, where people carved turnips and potatoes to ward off evil spirits.
🍬 Americans spend over $3 billion on Halloween candy each year — making it the second largest commercial holiday after Christmas.
🦇 Bats are a classic Halloween symbol partly because in the Middle Ages, large bonfires lit to ward off spirits attracted insects — and insects attracted bats.
🍂 October is the peak month for fall foliage in Colorado, making it one of the most beautiful months to live and work in Pagosa Springs.
October Trivia
What was the original name for Halloween?
Answer: Samhain, an ancient Celtic festival marking the end of harvest season and the beginning of winter.
