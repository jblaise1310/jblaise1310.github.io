This Month's FocusSelf-Care in Every Dimension

Self-care is not selfish — it is sustainable. It looks different for everyone, and it shows up in more areas of life than we often realize. Here are simple, meaningful ways to care for yourself across six key arenas.

🧠

Mental & Emotional

Give your mind permission to rest and reset.

  • Journal for 5 minutes each morning
  • Name one thing you're grateful for daily
  • Limit doom-scrolling to 15 min/day
  • Try a free meditation app like Insight Timer
  • Say "no" to one thing this week without guilt
🚶

Physical

Movement is medicine for the mind and body.

  • Take a 10-minute walk outside each day
  • Stretch for 5 minutes before bed
  • Drink a glass of water before coffee
  • Prioritize 7-8 hours of sleep
  • Try one new vegetable this month
🤝

Social & Relational

Connection is a basic human need, not a luxury.

  • Call or text one person you've been missing
  • Join a community group or class
  • Share how you really are with a trusted friend
  • Set a kind but clear boundary
  • Write a note of appreciation to someone
✨

Spiritual & Purposeful

Meaning and purpose sustain us through hard times.

  • Spend time in nature intentionally
  • Revisit a value that matters to you
  • Volunteer for something you believe in
  • Practice a moment of silence or prayer
  • Do one thing purely for joy this week
💼

Professional & Financial

Stress at work and around money affects mental health deeply.

  • Take your full lunch break away from your desk
  • Identify one work task you can delegate
  • Check your benefits for an EAP
  • Review one recurring expense this month
  • Name one professional win from this week
🎨

Creative & Playful

Play is not just for children — it heals adults too.

  • Draw, doodle, or color for 10 minutes
  • Cook a new recipe just for fun
  • Listen to music you loved as a teenager
  • Watch something lighthearted, guilt-free
  • Rearrange one small corner of your home

Daily PracticesSmall Habits, Big Impact

😮‍💨

Breathe on Purpose

Box breathing (4 counts in, hold, out, hold) activates the nervous system's rest response in under 2 minutes. It works anywhere, anytime.

Try it right now
📵

Create a Phone-Free Window

Even 30 minutes without your phone — first thing in the morning or before bed — measurably reduces anxiety and improves sleep quality.

Start with 30 minutes
🌤️

Get Morning Light

10 minutes of natural light in the morning regulates your circadian rhythm, improves mood, and supports better sleep at night. No gym required.

Step outside early
💬

Talk to Someone You Trust

Simply naming how you feel out loud to another person reduces the intensity of that feeling. You don't need advice — just to be heard.

One honest conversation

Resources for Older AdultsElderly Mental Health & Well-Being

Older adults are often overlooked in mental health conversations.

Depression and anxiety in adults over 65 are frequently mistaken for "normal aging." Isolation, loss of independence, grief, and chronic illness all elevate mental health risk in older adults — and they deserve support just as much as anyone.

National Resources
📞

Eldercare Locator

A nationwide service connecting older adults and caregivers to local support services, including mental health resources.

eldercare.acl.gov  ·  1-800-677-1116
🏠

AARP Mental Health Resources

Articles, tools, and a caregiver helpline for adults 50+ navigating mental health, grief, loneliness, and caregiving stress.

aarp.org/health/mental-health
🤝

Senior Companion Program

Connects isolated older adults with trained volunteer companions to reduce loneliness and support independence.

americorps.gov
🧠

Geriatric Mental Health Foundation

Resources on depression, anxiety, dementia, and mental wellness specifically for older adults and their families.

gmhfonline.org
