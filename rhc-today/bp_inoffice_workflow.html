In-Office Blood Pressure Averaging

Clinical Staff Reference Guide
Effective date: 06/01/26 Prepared by: Jo Blaise

Purpose

This workflow standardizes how in-office blood pressure readings are obtained, compared, and entered into the patient record. Consistent BP documentation supports accurate hypertension management and improves our performance on the CMS 165 Controlling High Blood Pressure eCQM measure.

Workflow

  1. Take the first BP reading at the beginning of the visit.
  2. If the reading is <140/90 mmHg — chart it. No second reading needed.
  3. If the reading is >140/90 mmHg — write it down but do not enter it in the chart. Take a second reading.
  4. From the two readings, chart the lower systolic and the lower diastolic.

Process for averaging BPs in clinic setting

Reading 1 — Do not chart
150/92
Reading 2
142/88

Select the lower value from each:

Systolic: lower of 150 and 142 = 142 Diastolic: lower of 92 and 88 = 88
