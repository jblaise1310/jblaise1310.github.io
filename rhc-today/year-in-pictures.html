Before you send: keep it patient-free

This digital photo book will be shared with the team, so please check every photo first.

  • No patients in any photo, even in the background.
  • No patient information visible: screens, charts, schedules, whiteboards, labels, or paperwork.
  • Ask first. Make sure everyone in the photo is okay with it being in the book.

How to send your photos.

It only takes a minute.

Pick your favorites

Choose the clearest version of each photo. Original files from your phone look best in the book.

Text or email them to Jo Blaise

Whatever is easiest for you! Texting works great, since you already have Jo's number. Or email jo.blaise@psmedicalcenter.org with the subject line Year in Pictures. Have a lot? Send them a few at a time so nothing gets stuck.

Add a quick caption

In your text or email, tell us who's in the photo and what was happening. A sentence is plenty.
