2026 RHC Organizational KPIs

Top-Line Goals Aligned to PSMC Strategic Priorities

These three KPIs define our highest-level targets for the year. Everything we do in our initiatives supports these outcomes.

1
📉 No-Show Reduction

Reduce Primary Care No-Shows by 40–50%

By the end of Q4, reduce RHC primary care no-shows by 40–50% compared to the Q1 benchmark, driven by continuous PDSA-based process improvement.

2
📋 Pre-Visit Paperwork

50% Pre-Visit Paperwork Compliance by Dec 31

Achieve 50% compliance with new and MAWV patients submitting required pre-visit paperwork at least 7 days prior to their scheduled visit by December 31, 2026.

3
⭐ Value-Based Program

Maintain Tier 3 Status in RMHP ACC 3.0

Maintain Tier 3 status by identifying two QI initiatives in Q1 with measurable goals, running ongoing PDSA cycles through Q2–Q4, and actively participating in all required meetings to maximize available funding.
