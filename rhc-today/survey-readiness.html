Survey Ready

Regulatory inspectors may visit our facility at any time — without advance notice. This guide helps every team member feel confident and prepared.

📋

What Is a Survey?

Regulatory agencies — such as CDPHE (Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment), state health departments, or CMS — have the authority to walk through our facility unannounced at any time. They observe our environment, review processes, and may ask staff direct questions about policies, safety, and patient care. Every team member — regardless of role — may be approached.

💡

It's OK to Say "I Don't Know" — Here's What to Do

If a surveyor asks you a question you are unsure about, it is completely acceptable to say:

"I don't know the specific details off the top of my head, but I can find that in our HealthStream Policy Manager."

Never guess or make up an answer. Honesty and knowing where to find the right information is exactly what surveyors respect.

Environment of Care & Facilities

🏥

Work Areas & Nurse Stations

  • Staff wear visible ID badges at all times
  • No open food or drinks at nurse stations
  • No patient health information visible to the public
  • Hand sanitizer stations are full and functioning
  • Workstations are clean, organized, and clutter-free
🚪

Hallways & Storage

  • Hallways are clear — items pushed to one side
  • Stairwells are free of clutter
  • Emergency exits are fully accessible
  • No bagged linen stored in hallways
  • Nothing stored within 18 inches of sprinkler heads
  • Nothing stored under sinks
🪑

Facilities Condition

  • Ceiling tiles are intact and clean
  • Walls are free of damage or chipped paint
  • Countertops have no chips or cracked laminate
  • Furniture is clean, intact, and professional
  • Handicap access points are functional and unobstructed
  • Medical gas shut-off valves are labeled and clear
🧴

Hand Hygiene

  • All dispensers are full and not expired
  • Report empty or malfunctioning dispensers to Facilities immediately
  • Include hand hygiene in safety huddles when relevant
2

Infection Control

🦠

Supplies & Storage

  • Supplies stored off the floor on solid shelving (no wire bottoms)
  • No expired items — random supply checks are expected
  • Clean and dirty workflows are clearly separated
  • Clean utility rooms contain only clean items
  • Clean linen stored in covered containers or closed cabinets
  • Isolation signage present where appropriate
🧪

Hazardous Materials

  • Red biohazard containers are covered and secured
  • Sharps containers are mounted and not overfilled
  • Spill kits (Red Z) are available and visible
  • All chemical containers are properly labeled
  • Chemicals stored separately from clean items or patient supplies
  • PPE available at chemical handling stations
  • Eyewash stations inspected weekly and clearly labeled
🔒

Compressed Gas Cylinders

  • All cylinders must be chained or in approved carts
  • Clearly labeled: "In Use," "Full," or "Empty"
  • No unlabeled or unsecured cylinders in patient care areas
3

Patient Privacy & HIPAA

🔏

No Patient Health Information (PHI) Visible to the Public

  • Lock your computer screen before stepping away
  • Avoid placing monitors where screens face hallways or waiting areas
  • Store encounter forms, labels, and printouts in covered bins — never on open counters
  • Keep papers facedown or out of public sight
  • Quick test: Before leaving a space, ask — "If a surveyor or patient walked by, could they see anything linking health info to a name?"
  • Remember: Even overheard conversations can be a HIPAA concern if they identify a patient

Safety Reporting — Know How to Speak Up

📣

Every Staff Member Should Know How To:

  • Report broken or defective medical equipment
  • Report an overfilled sharps container
  • Access SDS (Safety Data Sheets) and explain how to use them
  • Report a workplace safety concern
  • Report anything that doesn't seem right
👩‍💼

Who to Inform

  • Report concerns to Clinic Leadership
  • Safety Data Sheets (SDS) are available to all staff — know where to find them
  • When in doubt, speak up — no concern is too small
📖

For any policy details you're unsure about, the answer is always in HealthStream Policy Manager. Surveyors appreciate staff who know where to find the right information — you don't need to have it memorized.
