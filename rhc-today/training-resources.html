Deadline: September 30, 2026

MOAB — Management of Aggressive Behavior

MOAB is a nationally recognized program that equips healthcare staff with skills to safely manage challenging or escalating patient behaviors. Sessions are available monthly beginning April 2026. Early completion is strongly encouraged — Q4 becomes increasingly busy with evaluations and operational priorities.

Focus Area
Early recognition of agitation & escalation
Focus Area
Verbal de-escalation techniques
Focus Area
Personal & patient safety
Focus Area
Professional communication during difficult interactions
How to Enroll in HealthStream
  1. Log into HealthStream from your desktop icon
  2. Select Catalog from the top menu
  3. Search "MOAB"
  4. Choose the class that matches your assignment (see tables below)
