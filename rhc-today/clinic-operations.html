Law Enforcement & Immigration Requests

⚖️ Staff Guidance — Colorado Law Requirements

A new Colorado law outlines strict requirements for how healthcare staff must respond to law enforcement or immigration-related requests. Intentional noncompliance may result in significant fines to the individual and the hospital.

When approached by law enforcement or immigration officers:

Step 1
Do not provide information or allow access
Step 2
Do not confirm patient presence or status
Step 3
Ask officers to wait in a public area
Step 4
Immediately escalate to leadership using the approved script
