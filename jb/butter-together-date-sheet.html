The Bake

High-altitude savory biscuits

Makes 8 · ~7,000 ft

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour2 cups
  • Sugar1 tbsp
  • Baking powder2½ tsp
  • Baking soda¼ tsp
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Cold unsalted butter, cubed½ cup
  • Cold buttermilk¾ cup + 2 tbsp
  • Melted butter, for brushing1 tbsp
Optional fold-in
  • Sharp cheddar, grated½ cup
  • Chives, chopped2 tbsp

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F and line a sheet with parchment. The hot oven matters at altitude.
  2. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Cut in the cold butter to coarse crumbs with pea-sized bits. Fold in cheddar and chives now if using.
  4. Stir in the buttermilk just until shaggy. Don't overmix.
  5. Pat into a rectangle, fold over itself 3 to 4 times, then pat to about 1 inch thick.
  6. Cut straight down, no twisting, and place the biscuits close together.
  7. Bake 12 to 15 minutes until tall and golden. Start checking at 12.
  8. Brush the hot tops with melted butter and rest 5 minutes before serving.

The Pour

What to drink with it

No bloody marys in sight. Three easy options that play well with sausage, cheddar, and honey.

🍺
Crisp & clean
A cold pilsner or lager. Cuts the richness without competing with the food.
🍏
Apple-y & bright
A dry hard cider. Lovely against the salty bacon and sharp cheddar.
🍊
A little fancy
A paloma: tequila, grapefruit, lime, soda. Refreshing and easygoing.

No-proof and just as good: sparkling water with grapefruit and lime, or a non-alcoholic cider over ice.
