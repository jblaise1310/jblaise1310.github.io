💡 The Only Real Lever: Guest Count
With this venue's pricing structure, the per-person rate is fixed — there is no menu combination that brings 75 guests within a $12K all-in budget. The only meaningful way to hit the target is to reduce the guest count. At ~50 guests, the Peridot package + Beer/Wine/Margarita bar + 1 chef attendant lands at approximately $11,900–$12,200 after all charges — right at the target. Bottom Line for Sommer & Hayve
🌸 🌼 💜 🌸 🌼
The El Chorro 2027 menu is beautiful — but its pricing, mandatory service charges, and required chef attendants make a $10K–$12K all-in wedding difficult at 75 guests. The most realistic path to the budget is ~50 guests, which brings the venue F&B total to approximately $11,350 after all charges.

If the guest count stays at 75, the honest all-in venue number is closer to $17,000–$18,000, and the couple should plan their total wedding budget accordingly. The Peridot Stations package remains the best value on the menu — with the Beer, Wine & Margarita bar for cocktail hour and dinner, switching to a cash bar afterward.

A smaller, intimate gathering at El Chorro will feel just as magical — and be fully within reach.
