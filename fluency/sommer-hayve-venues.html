Virginia's House
🌿 Garden Venue Only ✓ Budget Fit 📍 Glendale, AZ (15 min from Phoenix)

A historic 1913 home on the National Register of Historic Places, transformed into Glendale's first boutique event venue. Set on a lush half-acre of blooming gardens with a 2,100 sq ft covered patio, gazebo, mature trees, seasonal flowers, and courtyard. Family-owned and operated since 1998. The venue rental includes everything — tables, chairs, linens, centerpieces, décor, full setup and cleanup — with no service charges, no gratuities, and no hidden fees. You bring your own caterer and bar service, keeping you in full control of the remaining budget.

  • 5-hour reception time block (Fri/Sat evenings or Sat mornings)
  • Tables, chairs, linens, centerpieces & décor all included
  • Full setup and cleanup — no extra staff fees
  • No service charges or gratuities added
  • Bring your own caterer and bar service
  • Up to 125 guests capacity — 50 guests is a comfortable fit
  • Off-season, Sunday & mid-week discounts available
  • First responder & military discounts offered

Secret Garden by Wedgewood Weddings
🌿 Garden All-Inclusive ✓ Budget Fit (off-peak) 📍 South Phoenix, AZ

A stunning historic estate in South Central Phoenix at the foot of South Mountain. Towering mesquite trees, date palms, native and tropical gardens, an iron cupola, carved stone altar, alfresco dining patio, indoor manor, and bistro lights. The Wedgewood all-inclusive package bundles food, beverage, coordinator, bar, linens, and rentals — you can arrive on the day and enjoy. Starting at $6,040 for off-peak dates, this can land within the $12K budget for 50 guests, particularly on a Sunday through Thursday booking.

  • All-inclusive: meal, 5-hour event time, tables, chairs, linens, coordination, bar, cake-cutting
  • Day-of coordination and event planning included
  • In-house bar and catering — no vendor sourcing needed
  • Multiple garden spaces: lawn, indoor manor, alfresco patio with bistro lights
  • Up to 200 guests capacity
  • Off-peak dates (June–Aug) and weekday pricing available
  • Short-term booking discount: $1,000 off if booked within 6 months
  • 4.9 stars — 150+ reviews on The Knot

Boojum Tree Hidden Gardens
🌿 Tropical Garden All-Inclusive Phoenix, AZ 📍 North Phoenix, AZ

A private five-acre tropical oasis in the heart of Phoenix with a 20-foot waterfall, forest of trees, ponds, fountains, and a tranquil river. The Bird of Paradise package is the all-inclusive option — DJ/MC, full catering by Heidi's Events, and a 5-hour premium open bar (vodka, whiskey, gin, tequila, rum, scotch, wine, beer, champagne toast) are all included. The setting is genuinely extraordinary and unlike any other Phoenix venue. At 50 guests on a Sunday–Thursday, the 2021 price was ~$12,295 before tax — current pricing will be higher and should be confirmed directly.

  • Bird of Paradise: DJ/MC + catering + 5-hour full premium open bar included
  • Heidi's Events catering: 2 passed hors d'oeuvres, fruit & cheese, salad, bread, vegetables, 2 entrées
  • Exclusive use of entire 5-acre facility — Outdoor Plaza, Indoor Tropical Garden, Gazebo Garden, Cantina, Arbor
  • Day-of coordinator, 125+ candles, all furniture, setup/teardown, security
  • 5-hour reception time block
  • 4.9 stars — 220+ reviews on WeddingWire
  • Orange Blossom (venue only) also available at lower cost

Desert Botanical Garden
🌵 Desert Garden Venue Only Phoenix, AZ 📍 Papago Park, Phoenix, AZ

140 acres of stunning Sonoran Desert landscape with 50,000 plant displays. The Steele Herb Garden is the ideal intimate space for 50 guests — scents of sage and lavender, views of Camelback Mountain and the Arizona sunset. As a non-profit, venue rental fees have no tax added. The tradeoff: tables, chairs, and linens are not provided and must come through your caterer; you must use their preferred caterer list; and you source all other vendors yourself. The lowest venue fee of any option — leaving the most budget for food and experience.

  • Steele Herb Garden: intimate setting for up to 75 guests, $2,600 rental fee
  • No tax on venue rental (non-profit institution)
  • No food or beverage minimums
  • Must use preferred caterer list for food (open to any other vendor)
  • Tables, chairs & linens not included — source through caterer
  • Access to garden grounds for guest strolling and photos
  • Luminaria sets, candle trees, lounge furniture available to rent as add-ons
  • 4.8 stars — 200+ reviews; award-winning venue

A Note on March 2027 Timing
March is peak season in Phoenix — the most beautiful weather of the year and the most in-demand dates. All four venues above will book out well in advance for March Saturdays. Virginia's House and Desert Botanical Garden offer the clearest path to staying within the $10K–$12K venue budget for 50 guests. Secret Garden by Wedgewood is the best all-in option if simplicity matters most. Boojum Tree is the most extraordinary setting but will likely require a budget conversation. Contact all venues directly to confirm current 2027 pricing and availability for March 12th.
