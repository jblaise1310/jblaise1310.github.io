Day-of Bar Strategy
Maximize the hosted hours · minimize overspend
Guest Arrival · Hour 1
🍾 Hosted Bar Opens
Beer, Wine & Margarita package begins as guests arrive. Passed hors d'oeuvres circulate, champagne toast is poured. All included.
Dinner Service · Hour 2
🌮 Stations Open · Bar Continues
Dinner stations open. The 2-hour hosted bar covers cocktail hour through dinner. Sticky buns, coffee, and tea service included.
After Dinner · Dancing
💃 Switch to Cash Bar
Hosted bar closes after dinner. Guests who want to continue drinking pay individually at a cash bar. This is the single biggest cost-control move of the evening.
Optional Add · From Your $649 Cushion
🌸 Welcome Drink Option
If desired, add a welcome cocktail at $14/person ($700 for 50 guests before tax) — this slightly exceeds the cushion, so ask the venue about a non-alcoholic welcome drink as an alternative.
