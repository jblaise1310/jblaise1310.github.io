Performance Analysis — David Albue
CMS-2 Depression Screening   |   Year-to-Date 2026

Your Strengths

  • Your screening rate has exceeded the 69.9% benchmark every month this year — January 85.0%, February 81.0%, March 83.0%, April 76.0%. This kind of consistent performance is exactly what the measure demands.
  • Your YTD screening rate of 81.2% (622 of 766 eligible patients) puts you 11.3 percentage points above benchmark — among the strongest performances in the clinic.
  • Your communication rate for patients scoring 5 or higher is strong and has improved month over month: January 80.0% → February 87.0% → March 91.0% → April 95.0%. Following up with higher-scoring patients is one of the most impactful parts of this measure.
  • In April you had 212 eligible patients and maintained a strong screening rate — showing your workflow holds up even with higher volume.

Your Areas for Improvement

  • Your minor screening rate (ages 12–17) is 47.4% YTD (9 of 19 qualifying patients). While there is no separate benchmark, bringing this closer to your overall rate would strengthen the measure.

Your Recommendations for Improvement

  • Keep doing what you are doing on overall screening — your intake workflow is clearly working and your numbers reflect that.
  • Maintain your strong communication follow-up for high-scoring patients. If capacity allows, consider sharing your approach with colleagues whose communication rates are lower.
  • For your minor patients, make it a habit to confirm the PHQ-A tool is offered at every qualifying visit for ages 12–17. Even one or two additional screenings per month can meaningfully improve your minor rate given the smaller pool.
