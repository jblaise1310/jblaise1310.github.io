Performance Analysis — Jennifer Carr
CMS-2 Depression Screening   |   Year-to-Date 2026

Your Strengths

  • Your screening rate has been trending in the right direction month over month — January 63.0%, February 50.6%, March 51.0%, April 68.0%. That upward momentum is encouraging and worth sustaining.

Your Areas for Improvement

  • Your YTD screening rate of 57.6% (182 of 316 eligible patients) is 12.3 percentage points below the 69.9% benchmark. This is the primary area to focus on for the remainder of the year.
  • Your communication rate for patients scoring 5 or higher is 0.0% YTD, which is below the 69.9% benchmark. Ensuring every patient with a score of 5+ receives a documented follow-up will help close this gap.
  • Your minor screening rate (ages 12–17) is 28.0% YTD (7 of 25 qualifying patients). While there is no separate benchmark, bringing this closer to your overall rate would strengthen the measure.

Your Recommendations for Improvement

  • Focus on closing the gap to the 69.9% benchmark. Review your intake process to identify which encounter types or patient segments are most commonly missed, and work with your team to build in a screening prompt at the start of each visit.
  • Prioritize follow-up documentation for patients who score 5 or higher on the screening tool. Even a brief documented communication attempt counts toward the measure and can make a meaningful difference in your rate.
  • For your minor patients, make it a habit to confirm the PHQ-A tool is offered at every qualifying visit for ages 12–17. Even one or two additional screenings per month can meaningfully improve your minor rate given the smaller pool.
