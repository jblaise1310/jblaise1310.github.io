Provider Actions

Active quality measures and the required provider actions needed to meet performance standards.

This document outlines active quality measures and the required provider actions necessary to meet performance standards.
CMS-2: Depression Screening and Follow-Up Plan 12 PTS

Percentage of patients age 12 and older who are screened for depression using a standardized tool (PHQ-2/PHQ-9) and, if positive, have a documented follow-up plan on the same date of service.

Provider Required Actions:

PHQ-9 Score 1–4 PHQ-9 Score 5–9 PHQ-9 Score ≥10
  • No action required
  • Clinical discretion for follow-up
  • Consider adding diagnosis when appropriate
 Provider must complete and document at least one of the following:
  • Behavioral Health referral
  • Initiate or adjust a common depression medication
  • Engage Integrated Behavioral Health
CSSRS: Suicide Risk Screening HQIP Zero Suicide

All patients are screened for suicide risk using the CSSRS tool. Any positive finding requires documented provider assessment and action prior to discharge. Screenings: All patients (screened every visit).

Provider Required Actions:

Negative Screen Low Risk Moderate Risk High Risk
No action required Place Mental Health referral Provider must take and document clinical action, which may include:
  • Behavioral Health consultation
  • Documented Safety Plan
  • Consider ED transfer
 Patient must not be discharged without documented intervention:
  • Safety Plan and Behavioral Health consult or ED transfer required
CMS-155a: Weight Assessment & BMI 4 PTS

Percentage of patients age 3–17 with documented BMI percentile and counseling for nutrition and physical activity during the measurement period.

Provider Required Action:

No provider action required
CMS-122: Diabetes, Hemoglobin A1c 6 PTS

Poor Control (>9%). Percentage of patients age 18–75 with diabetes whose most recent A1c is greater than 9%, or who have no documented A1c during the measurement period. Lower performance percentage reflects better control.

Provider Required Actions:

If A1c >9% or missing:
  • Inform CSS to obtain A1c during visit
  • Other therapies / medications per provider discretion
CMS-951: Kidney Health Evaluation in Patients with Diabetes 4 PTS

Percentage of patients age 18–85 with diabetes who received a kidney health evaluation during the measurement period, defined as both an eGFR and a uACR completed with quantitative results, once per year.

Provider Required Actions:

  • Order Microalb/Creat Ratio (uACR) alongside the CMP for diabetic patients if not yet completed this year
  • eGFR is typically captured with the CMP already; confirm both are resulted
CMS-165: Controlling High Blood Pressure 12 PTS

Percentage of patients age 18–85 with a diagnosis of hypertension whose most recent blood pressure during the measurement period is less than 140/90 mmHg.

Provider Required Action:

If BP ≥140/90 during the visit:
  • Direct and document a follow-up plan for blood pressure management
  • Communicate need for BP recheck prior to discharge
CMS-146: Testing for Pharyngitis 4 PTS

Percentage of patients age 3 and older diagnosed with pharyngitis who were prescribed an antibiotic and received a Group A streptococcus (strep) test on or within 3 days of the visit.

Provider Required Actions:

  • Order a Strep Test when entering a diagnosis of pharyngitis AND prescribing antibiotics
CMS-154: Treatment for URI 4 PTS

Percentage of patients age 3 months and older diagnosed with upper respiratory infection (URI) who were NOT prescribed an antibiotic. This is an inverse measure: lower antibiotic prescribing reflects better performance.

Provider Required Actions:

  • For patients diagnosed with a URI, do not prescribe antibiotics
Additional Measures (not included in Strategic Measures above)
CMS-125: Breast Cancer Screening 6 PTS

Percentage of female patients age 50–74 who have received a mammogram within the past 27 months.

Recommended Provider Action:

  • Order screening mammogram when indicated
  • Document if patient declines or screening is contraindicated
CMS-130: Colorectal Cancer Screening 6 PTS

Percentage of patients age 50–75 who have appropriate colorectal cancer screening documented (FIT within 12 months, colonoscopy within 10 years, or other approved methods).

Recommended Provider Action:

  • Order appropriate colorectal cancer screening when indicated
  • Document if patient declines or screening is contraindicated
