The Big Three: Forecasting Our Comeback Trail

Blood pressure, A1c, and kidney health are our three highest-stakes stories right now: 24 combined points, two active PDSA cycles, and one very promising trend line. Here's where each one is actually headed, math included.

🤝

Why A1c and Kidney Health are basically workflow siblings

Both measures live in the same patient population: people with diabetes. Every visit where a provider tightens A1c control is also a natural moment to order the uACR and eGFR that Kidney Health needs. Improve the diabetes visit once, and both numbers move. That's not a coincidence — it's the same appointment doing double duty.

CMS-165Controlling High Blood Pressure

12 pts Trend paused

The heavyweight of the point pool, and the one playing the longest game.

Where we are

63.48% in June, up +12.6 percentage points since January but essentially flat the last two months (63.71% → 63.68% → 63.48%). Still 12.2 points short of the 75.71% benchmark.

What we're not doing

We're not projecting a benchmark date here, on purpose. The in-office and home BP-averaging workflow only launched 6/1/26, and this eCQM's denominator rolls on a lag — June's rate barely reflects three weeks of the new process.

PDSA status: Active. STUDY and ACT conclusions stay "too early to determine" until we have a full post-launch reporting cycle. The metric that actually tells us if the workflow is working is encounter-level F/U-BP adoption, tracked separately — that's the leading indicator to watch before this rate moves.

CMS-122Diabetes: A1c Poor Control >9%

6 pts Fastest mover

Lower is better here, and this measure has been on a diet since January.

Where we are

Poor control dropped from 61.01% in January to 24.41% in June — a 36.6 point improvement in six months, and the steepest trajectory of any measure on this page.

What the math says

At the recent 3-month pace, we cross the ≤21.50% benchmark as early as late July; at the more conservative recent month-over-month pace, closer to mid-August. Either way, it lines up almost exactly with the scheduled 8/31 evaluation.

PDSA status: Active, formal evaluation scheduled 8/31/26. This forecast is a trend projection, not a conclusion — the official STUDY/ACT read happens after the eval date, once we can see whether the pace held.

CMS-951Kidney Health Evaluation

4 pts Quiet achiever

No PDSA cycle, no fanfare, just a straight line up and to the right.

Where we are

From 6.25% in January to 34.55% in June — more than a fivefold increase, with zero new systems and zero new staff. Just providers building it into the visit.

What the math says

Holding the recent 3-month pace of roughly +3.5 points a month, this measure crosses the 54.15% benchmark around late November 2026. If the A1c workflow keeps compounding with it, that date could move up.

Coaching status: Not yet under a formal PDSA cycle — currently coached through routine huddles. Given the trajectory, it may be worth formalizing once CMS-122 wraps its evaluation, so both diabetes-linked measures share one cycle.

Eight points is not a mountain. It's a hallway.

Six of eleven measures are already at benchmark. The two carrying the most points, blood pressure and A1c, are both moving, one loudly and one quietly. Kidney health is doing its own thing in the background and doing it well. None of this happened by accident, and none of it needs to slow down.

Keep going. The numbers are listening.
