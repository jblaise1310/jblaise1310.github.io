28 points of momentum

In January, one in sixteen patients with diabetes or hypertension had their kidney labs completed. By June, more than one in three did.

No new system. No new staff. Just providers who were asked to do something, and did it.

Kidney health is one of the quietest complications in primary care — it progresses without symptoms until it doesn’t. Every uACR ordered, every eGFR checked, is a patient who gets to know before they have to find out the hard way.

At the current rate of improvement, this measure reaches benchmark by November 2026.

That is a real number. That is this team.

Keep going.
