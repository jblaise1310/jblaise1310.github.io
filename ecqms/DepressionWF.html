Depression Screening (PHQ) CSSRS Screening
PHQ Score Response Protocol

Depression Screening — Scoring Options

1–4 Low Score
No further action required
5–9 Moderate Score
Follow-up per provider's discretion
Providers are advised to designate depression-related diagnoses as chronic whenever applicable
10+ High Score
Follow-up per provider's discretion
Designate depression-related diagnoses as chronic whenever applicable
Complete one or more of the following:
  1. Place an order for Referral Management to medical service: RM Behavioral Health
  2. Place a prescription order (common medications used for depression will qualify)
  3. Contact Integrated BH
📄
To include the depression screening in your clinic note, use auto-text: PHQ-DepressionScreening
⚠ MIPS Exclusion Documentation Reminder

When a patient declines or is unable to complete the depression screening, this must be documented using the MIPS exclusion field in the chart. Without this documentation, there is no way to distinguish between a patient who refused screening and a patient who was never offered it. This step is required and directly impacts your quality measure performance data.
