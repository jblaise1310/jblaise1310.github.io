The Code to Use
F/U BP

When scheduling a patient whose visit includes a blood pressure assessment or hypertension follow up, the Reason for Visit must be coded as F/U BP. This is how the measure captures that a BP follow up occurred. Without it, the visit may not count.

Quick Reference
✓ Use
✓Reason for Visit: F/U BP
✓Enter at scheduling before the visit
✓Confirm at rooming if visit includes a BP check
✗ Avoid
✗Generic codes: F/U, Office Visit, Follow up
✗Leaving RFV blank or using a non BP reason
✗Assuming it will be corrected later
