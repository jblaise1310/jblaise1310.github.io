Risk Level Response Protocol

CSSRS Screening — Provider Actions by Risk Level

Negative Q1, Q2 & Q6 all negative
No further action required
Low Risk Q1 and/or Q2 positive
Place a RM Mental Health referral
Additional actions, if warranted, per provider's clinical judgement new
Medium Risk Q3 and/or Q6 positive
Use clinician judgement with immediate BH consultation or ED transfer new
At minimum: written and signed Safety Plan + RM Mental Health
If PSMC BH not available: request Tele-Psych consult. Clinic patients must be transferred to the ED for Tele-Psych.
High Risk Q4, Q5 and/or Q7 positive
Use clinician judgement with immediate BH consultation or ED transfer new
At minimum: written and signed Safety Plan + RM Mental Health
If PSMC BH not available: request Tele-Psych consult. Clinic patients must be transferred to the ED.
ℹ Definition — "Immediate BH Consultation": The patient is not discharged to home and remains under PSMC care. It does not indicate that BH must or will see the patient immediately.
CSSRS Question Reference

Screening Questions & Risk Mapping

Question 1 — Low Risk Trigger
In past month, have you wished you were dead or wished you could go to sleep and not wake up?
Question 2 — Low Risk Trigger
In past month, have you actually had thoughts about killing yourself?
Question 3 — Medium Risk Trigger
In past month, have you been thinking about how you might kill yourself?
Question 4 — High Risk Trigger
In past month, have you had these thoughts and had some intention of acting on them?
Question 5 — High Risk Trigger
In past month, have you started to work out or worked out details of how to kill yourself? Do you intend to carry out this plan?
Question 6a/6b — Med/Low Trigger
6a (Lifetime): Have you ever done anything, started to do anything, or prepared to do anything to end your life?

6b (Past 3 months): Same question scoped to past 3 months.
Branching logic: If YES to Question 2 — ask questions 3, 4, 5, 6a, and 6b.  |  If NO to Question 2 — go directly to Question 6a.

Behavioral Health Referral Actions

  • 1Item 1 positive — Behavioral Health Referral
  • 2Item 2 positive — Behavioral Health Referral
  • 3Item 3 positive — BH Consult (Psych Nurse/Social Worker) and consider Patient Safety Precautions
  • 4Item 4 positive — BH Consultation and Patient Safety Precautions
  • 5Item 5 positive — BH Consultation and Patient Safety Precautions

Lifetime & Recent Actions

  • 6aItem 6a positive (Lifetime) — BH Consult (Psych Nurse/Social Worker) and consider Patient Safety Precautions
  • 6bItem 6b positive (3 months or less) — BH Consultation and Patient Safety Precautions
  • 7Item 7 positive — High Risk trigger; use clinician judgement for immediate BH consultation or ED transfer
Documentation Reference

Reviewing CSSRS Charting & Provider Communication

📄 To review a charted CSSRS

  • 1Navigate the Documents component
  • 2Click the row of the most recent nursing intake (e.g., Adult Comprehensive Intake)
  • 3Scroll down the note to locate the CSSRS documentation

🔔 Notified of a positive screen or refusal?

  • 1Navigate the Documents component
  • 2Click the row of the Communication Book (date should match the encounter date)
  • 3See Reason Physician Notified for details of notification
⚠ MIPS Exclusion Documentation Reminder

When a patient declines or is unable to complete the CSSRS screening, this must be documented using the MIPS exclusion field in the chart. Without this documentation, there is no way to distinguish between a patient who refused screening and a patient who was never offered it. This step is required and directly impacts your quality measure performance data.
