Kidney Health in Diabetes (CMS-951)

A provider-led measure. Two lab tests, once a year, for every patient with diabetes. Here is what CSS should know.
Adults with diabetes 4 quality points eGFR + uACR, every year

The Kidney Health Evaluation (KED) measures whether patients with diabetes get both a kidney-function test (eGFR) and an early-damage test (uACR) each year. The ordering is provider-led. This sheet is so CSS understand what is being asked, and can help catch the gap.

Measure Overview

What KED requires each year

  • ✓The patient has diabetes.
  • ✓Both an eGFR and a uACR completed during the measurement year.
  • ✓Results are quantitative (numeric).
  • ✓One kidney health evaluation, once per year.

The two tests

  • ✓eGFR (estimated glomerular filtration rate) assesses kidney function. It rides along on the CMP, so it is rarely the problem.
  • ✓uACR (urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio) detects early albumin leakage, the earliest sign of kidney damage. This is the test that gets missed.
The gap is the uACR. We reliably order the eGFR because it comes with the CMP. The uACR (Microalb/Creat Ratio) is the test that gets missed, and that is the main driver of missed KED performance. The uACR catches early kidney damage even when the eGFR looks normal, so skipping it delays diagnosis and treatment.
When Providers Order It
Opportunity 1
Diabetic follow-up visits
Opportunity 2
Annual visits
Opportunity 3
Medicare Annual Wellness Visits

At each of these, the provider orders both tests if they are not yet done for the year, placing the uACR alongside the CMP. The MAWV is a good last chance to close the gap before the year ends.

The Order to Know
Microalb/Creat Ratio

The Cerner order name for the uACR. It goes in alongside the CMP for diabetic patients.

Where CSS Come In

A helpful reminder, at patient prep

The ordering is the provider's. But CSS reviewing charts ahead of the visit are well placed to catch the gap. During patient prep, before the visit, look for a patient with diabetes who has no uACR (Microalb/Creat Ratio) yet this year, and flag it for the provider so it can be ordered alongside the CMP.

“No uACR on file this year, want it added to today's labs?”

Catch it at prep, not at rooming, so the order goes in with the visit's other labs. It is a reminder, not a decision: the provider places the order.
